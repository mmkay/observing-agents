Note:
In comparison to metrics, the logs were surprisingly bad. By default, there was nothing in the logs that could become useful. On one hand, this is good - as logs are usually stored on the device, it's easier not to think about things like making sure the disk doesn't get full. It's also easy to keep some sensitive data in the logs. Logs, however, have given way to another, more powerful signal.
